SuperrBot Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Superlinear Technologies Private Limited

The short version

  • You sign in with an email and a password. We keep your account, your conversations and the files you send on our server so your bots can work for you.
  • To do that work, your conversations are processed by Anthropic's Claude, and the apps you connect are reached through Composio.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time, and everything stored for it on our server goes with it.

Who we are

SuperrBot is made by Superlinear Technologies Private Limited, a company in India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the SuperrBot app for iPhone and iPad, including its Share extension and widgets, and the SuperrBot service it signs in to. SuperrBot is currently in beta.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address. This is how you sign in and how we reach you about your account.
  • Password. We never store your password itself, only a one-way scrambled form of it (a salted scrypt hash), so not even we can read it.
  • A first name taken from your email address. Your bots use it to address you, and it is said when a bot phones someone on your behalf.
  • A device label for each phone you sign in on, so signed-in devices can be told apart.

What you send, and what your bots make

  • Messages and conversations with your bots, including the instructions you give each bot.
  • Photos, files, links and text you attach in a chat or send from another app's Share sheet.
  • What your bots produce: their replies, the steps they take, files they create, routines you schedule, and the questions and approvals they ask you.

All of this is stored on our server in a private space for your account, so your conversations are there whenever you open the app.

Apps you connect

If you connect an app such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Slack, Notion or GitHub, you sign in to that app directly and authorise access. The connection is held by our provider Composio, not by us. Your bots can then read and act in that app when you ask them to, and they ask you first before anything that sends, buys or deletes. You can disconnect an app at any time in the app's Connected apps screen.

Web browsing by your bots

When a bot browses the web for you, it uses a browser running on our server that can only reach the public internet. Website data the browser saves, such as cookies from a site the bot signed in to for you, is stored encrypted.

Phone calls

If you ask a bot to phone someone, the call is placed by our provider Bland AI. The bot introduces itself as your assistant. The call is recorded and transcribed, and the transcript and summary are saved to your account so you can see what was said. You decide who a bot calls.

Problem reports

If you send us a problem report from the app, it includes your message, your email, your account ID, the app version and your phone model and iOS version.

On your phone

  • Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you dictate a message or talk to a bot. Speech is turned into text on your phone when your phone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service does it. We receive only the text, not your voice.
  • Camera is used only to scan a pairing code, if you choose to pair with a computer.
  • Local network is used only to find a computer to pair with on the same Wi-Fi, if you choose to.
  • SuperrBot does not access your contacts or your location.

Who processes your data

We use these providers to run SuperrBot. Each receives only what it needs for its part of the work, and some of them process data outside India, including in the United States.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.
  • We do not use your conversations to train AI models. Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its commercial API by default.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account, conversations and files for as long as you have an account, so your bots and their history are there when you come back. You can delete a conversation thread in the app at any time.

To delete everything, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account in the app. This removes your account, signs out every device, disconnects every app you connected, and deletes your conversations, files and everything else stored for your account on our server. The encryption key for your bots' saved website data is deleted too.

Problem reports you sent are kept separately so we can fix what went wrong. Email us and we will delete yours.

How we protect it

  • The app talks to our server only over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, and sign-in tokens only as hashes.
  • Each account runs in its own private space on our server, and your bots' browser cannot reach our internal network.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects it.

Your choices and rights

You can see and delete your conversations in the app, disconnect any app you connected, and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and we will reply within 30 days.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use SuperrBot.

Changes to this policy

SuperrBot is in beta and will change. When this policy changes, we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
